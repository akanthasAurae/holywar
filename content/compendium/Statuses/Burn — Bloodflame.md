<h1 class=bf>Burn — Bloodflame</h1>

> [!bf|status|none|no-mix] 
> <img src="bloodflame.png" class="floatl"> <span><span class=bf>Bloodflame</span> builds through three levels — <span class=bf>Minor</span>, <span class=bf>Moderate</span> and <span class=bf>Major</span>. Any <img src="bloodflame.png" class="icon"> <span class=bf>Bloodflame</span> applied while in <img src="bloodflame.png" class="icon"> <span class=bf>Major Bloodflame</span> immediately triggers <img src="enflame.png" class="icon"> <span class=bf>Enflamed</span>.</span>
> 
> When triggered, the unit gains ![[blood.png|icon]] [[Bloodloss|Moderate Bloodloss]] and becomes ![[seared.png|icon]] <span class=bf>Seared</span> for two ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]]. At the end of each of their ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]], gain ![[blood.png|icon]] [[Bloodloss|Minor Bloodloss]] and lose one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]]. If ![[enflame.png|icon]] <span class=bf>Enflamed</span> is triggered again while ![[seared.png|icon]] <span class=bf>Seared</span> is still active, the duration is refreshed.
> 
> *Unique ![[burn.png|icon]] [[Burn]].*
