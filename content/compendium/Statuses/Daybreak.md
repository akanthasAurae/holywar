<h1 class=daybreak>Daybreak</h1>

> [!daybroken|status|none|no-mix]
> <img src="daybroken.png" class=floatl> <span><span class=daybreak>Daybreak</span> builds through three levels — <span class=daybreak>Minor</span>, <span class=daybreak>Moderate</span>, and <span class=daybreak>Major</span>. Any <img src="daybroken.png" class=icon> <span class=daybreak>Daybreak</span> applied while in <img src="daybroken.png" class=icon> <span class=daybreak>Major Daybreak</span> immediately triggers <img src="eruption.png" class=icon> <span class=daybreak>Eruption</span>.</span>
> 
> When triggered, <img src="eruption.png" class=icon> <span class=daybreak>Eruption</span> forcibly inflicts 2 ![[stancebreak.png|icon]] [[Stancebroken]], ignoring any passives that would prevent its affliction.
> 
> Like ![[radiance.png|icon]] [[Radiance]], ![[daybroken.png|icon]] <span class=daybreak>Daybreak</span> decays at a rate of one level of ![[daybroken.png|icon]] <span class=daybreak>Daybreak</span> per ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions|Action]] taken, consuming one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States|Vital State]] per decayed level.
> 
> <i class="radiance no-b">"I burn with the wrath and scorn of a thousand suns! You will never fathom what I've endured, from disgrace to contempt!"</i>
> 
> *Unique ![[burn.png|icon]] [[Burn]] and ![[radiance.png|icon]] [[Radiance]].*
