<h1 class=venom>Toxin — Venom</h1>

> [!venom|status|none]
> <img src=venom.png class=floatl> <span><span class=venom>Venom</span> builds through three levels — <span class=ven>Minor</span>, <span class=venom>Moderate</span> and <span class=venom>Major</span>. Any <img src=venom.png class=icon> <span class=venom>Venom</span> applied while in <img src=venom.png class=icon> <span class=venom>Major Venom</span> immediately triggers <img src=envenomed.png class=icon> <span class=venom>Envenomed</span>.</span>
> 
> When triggered, the unit gains 4 ![[comdown.png|icon]] [[Combat Power Down]] and 4 ![[defdown.png|icon]] [[Defense Power Down]]. After two ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]], it will consume one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States|Vital State]], causing the unit to vomit and replacing the status with ![[intox.png|icon]] [[Toxin|Intoxicated]].
> 
> If ![[venom.png|icon]] <span class=venom>Venom</span> is applied while the target has ![[tox.png|icon]] [[Toxin]] or is ![[intox.png|icon]] [[Toxin|Intoxicated]], replace it with ![[venom.png|icon]] <span class=venom>Venom</span>.
> 
> *Unique ![[tox.png|icon]] [[Toxin]].*
