<h1 class="deathblight no-mix">Deathblight</h1>

> [!deathblight|status|none|no-mix]
> <img src="deathblight.png" class=floatl> <i class="deathblight">"Quote."</i>
> 
> ![[deathblight.png|icon]] <span class=deathblight>Deathblight</span> advances through three levels — <span class=deathblight>Minor</span>, <span class=deathblight>Moderate</span> and <span class=deathblight>Major</span>. Any ![[deathblight.png|icon]] <span class=deathblight>Deathblight</span> applied while in ![[deathblight.png|icon]] <span class=deathblight>Major Deathblight</span> triggers ![[deathblow.png|icon]] <span class=deathblight>Deathblow</span>.
> 
> When triggered, the unit becomes [[Downed|incapacitated]] for the rest of the encounter, unless freed, consuming four ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]]. If the unit has any remaining ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]], they will force themselves free.
> 
> After triggering, enemies cannot target this unit can it can be freed by allies. ![[deathblight.png|icon]] <span class=deathblight>Deathblight</span> decays by one level every two ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]].
