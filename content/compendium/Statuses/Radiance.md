<h1 class=radiance>Radiance</h1>

> [!radiance|status|none]
> <img src="radiance.png" class=floatl> <span><span class=radiance>Radiance</span> advances through three levels — <span class=radiance>Minor</span>, <span class=radiance>Moderate</span> and <span class=radiance>Major</span>. Any <img src="radiance.png" class=icon> <span class=radiance>Radiance</span> applied while in <img src="radiance.png" class=icon> <span class=radiance>Major Radiance</span> triggers <img class=icon src=blinded.png> <span class=blinded>Blinded</span>. Unlike many other status effects, <img src="radiance.png" class=icon> <span class=radiance>Radiance</span> does not expire when <img class=icon src=blinded.png> <span class=blinded>Blinded</span> is triggered.</span> 
> 
> When ![[blinded.png|icon]] <span class=blinded>Blinded</span> is triggered, the unit gains the following effects while it's active: <br>
> 5 ![[minmaxdown.png|icon]] [[Final Power Down]] <br>
> After performing an ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions|Action]], gain ![[tremor.png|icon]] [[Tremor|Minor Tremor]] <br>
> <span class=minus>[Clash Lose]</span> gain ![[tremor.png|icon]] [[Tremor|Minor Tremor]]
> 
> After triggering, ![[radiance.png|icon]] <span class=radiance>Radiance</span> decays by a level per ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions|Action]]. <br>Once  ![[radiance.png|icon]] <span class=radiance>Radiance</span> fully decays, ![[blinded.png|icon]] <span class=blinded>Blinded</span> will be removed. <br> ![[radiance.png|icon]] <span class=radiance>Radiance</span> cannot be stacked while ![[blinded.png|icon]] <span class=blinded>Blinded</span> is active.
>
