<h1 class=tox>Toxin</h1>

> [!tox|status|none]
> <img src=tox.png class="floatl"> <span><span class=tox>Toxin</span> builds through three levels — <span class=tox>Minor</span>, <span class=tox>Moderate</span>, and <span class=tox>Major</span>. Any <img src=tox.png class="icon"><span class=tox>Toxin</span> applied while in <span class=tox>Major Toxin</span> immediately triggers <img src=intox.png class="icon"> <span class=intox>Intoxicated</span>.</span>
> 
> When triggered, the unit gains 2 ![[comdown.png|icon]] [[Combat Power Down]] and 2 ![[defdown.png|icon]] [[Defense Power Down]]. After three ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]], it will consume one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States|Vital State]], causing the unit to vomit and expiring the status. If ![[tox.png|icon]] <span class=tox>Toxin</span> is applied while the unit is <span class=intox>Intoxicated</span>, refresh its duration.
