<h1 class=sanity>Sanity</h1>

> [!sanity|status|none]
> <img src="sanity.png" class="floatl"><span> Every character has <img src="sanity.png" class="icon"> <span class=sanity>Sanity</span>. It functions as a measure of one's current composure, rationality, and ability to mantain it in any given situation, ranging from <span class=san>Calm</span>, <span class=san2>Composed</span>, <span class=san3>Nervous</span>, <span class=san4>Delirious</span> or <span class=san5>Panicking</span>, and is determined by one's <span class=tech>Technicality</span> and <span class=intel>Intelligence</span>.</span>
> 
> ![[sanity.png|icon]] <span class=sanity>Sanity</span> is lost when a character suffers excessive mental damage or when it is explicitly removed by status effects or skills. When a character endures too much mental damage, they <span class=san5>panic</span>, entering either ![[psy.png|icon]] [[Psychosis]] or becoming ![[der.png|icon]] [[Deranged]], depending on the unit’s highest attribute.
> 
> Units that have higher <span class=intel>Intelligence</span> than <span class=tech>Technicality</span> enter ![[psy.png|icon]] [[Psychosis]] when their ![[sanity.png|icon]] <span class=sanity>Sanity</span> is depleted, and units that have higher <span class=tech>Technicality</span> than <span class=intel>Intelligence</span> become ![[der.png|icon]] [[Deranged]] instead.
