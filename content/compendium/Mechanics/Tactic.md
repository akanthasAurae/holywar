<h1 class=tac>Tactic</h1>

> [!tac|status|none]
> <img src="tactic.png" class=floatl> <span><span class=tac>Tactic</span> is the manifestation of one's <span class=tech>Technical</span> prowess, used to modify the duration of <img src=action.png class=icon> <a data-href="Actions" href="Actions" class="internal-link">Action-based</a> Status Effects such as <img src=burn.png class=icon> <a data-href="Burn" href="Burn" class="internal-link">Burn</a> and <img src=tox.png class=icon> <a data-href="Toxin" href="Toxin" class="internal-link">Toxin</a>.</span>
> 
> These Status Effects have conditions that can be fulfilled the higher your <span class=tac>Tactic</span> is, increasing or shortening the duration, depending on the Status Effect. These conditionals are divided in multiple tiers, starting at **tier 1**.
>
>The limit of how much a Status Effect's length can be upgraded depends on the Status Effect itself. Some effects have a limit of **tier 3**, while others can reach up to **tier 5**. 
>
>*(Additionally, to unlock higher tiers, the character must research their own effects to learn them better and thus achieving such results; be it field research, on-hands, or theoretical.)*
>
>A character can gain higher tiers, and thus, better durations, through the investment in <span class="tech">Technical</span>, increasing the tier for every <span class=tech>10 Technical</span>.
