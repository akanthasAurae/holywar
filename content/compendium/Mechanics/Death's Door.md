<h1 class="dd"> Death's Door </h1>

> [!dd|status|none] 
> <img src="dd.png" class="floatl"><i class="no-b dd no-mix">The bells are tolling. Your body pushes onwards, and your will ignores the call... at what cost?</i>
> 
> When this unit takes fatal damage, a check *(DC 5)* is rolled towards the **Body**.
> 
> If the unit succeeds, they permanently gain 4 ![[comdown.png|icon]] [[Combat Power Down]] and 4 ![[defdown.png|icon]] [[Defense Power Down]] for every successful check, for as long as this status is applied, and they continue to fight. The next DC is then increased by 5 *(stacks)*. If the unit fails, they will be ![[downed.png|icon]] [[Downed|incapacitated]] and can be <span class="dd">executed</span>.
> 
> If the unit recovers any ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]] while this status is active, it will expire and be replaced by ![[ddrec.png|18]] [[Death's Door — Recovery]].
