<h1 class="bleed">Unbreakable Skills</h1>

> [!bleed|status|none] 
>  <img src="unbreak.png" class="floatl"><span>Some Skills have a special kind of property called <img src=unbreak.png class=icon> <span class=bleed>Unbreakable</span>, which gives them the ability to be used regardless if the unit <span class=minus>lost</span> the clash. </span>
>   
>  These skills often have weaker **\[On Hit\]** effects, instead having stronger effects when <span class=plus>winning</span> a clash. The point of these rolls is to inflict a certain effect with guarantee, albeit being less effective when ![[cracked.png|icon]] <span class=hemo>Cracked</span>.
>   
>  When <span class=minus>losing</span> a clash, the unit will be struck by the enemy’s skill and then this unit will retaliate with their ![[cracked.png|icon]] <span class=hemo>Cracked Skill</span>. ![[cracked.png|icon]] <span class=hemo>Cracked Skills</span> themselves are incapable of removing ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States]] and ![[stance.png|icon]] [[Stance]], although Status Effects they apply still can.
