<h1 class=nimius>Nimius</h1>

> [!nimius|status|none] 
> <img src="nimius.png" class="floatl"> <span>The physical manifestation of <span class=nimius>demonic mana</span>, composed primarily from emotions, followed by one's sins. Attainable only to demons and demonic beings. Otherwise known as <span class=nimius>Nimius Vitia</span> or <span class=nimius>Vitia Aether</span>.</span>
> 
> Can be attained through certain items, passives and conditionals, and is often spent to make additional effects occur that can otherwise not be achieved by default. 
> 
> Possesses sub-types such as <span class=unsheath>Glacies</span>, <span class=neutral>Astoria</span>, <span class=igni>Insomus</span>, <span class=scorch>Cupidia</span>, <span class=tox>Avaritium</span>, <span class=rolls>Invidies</span>, <span class=down>Ignatian</span>, <span class=venom>Gulavies</span>, and <span class=hemo>Iracun</span>. The most known sub-type, due to the Holy War, is <span class=deathblight>Morspes</span>, famously known as <span class=deathblight>Deathblight</span>.
