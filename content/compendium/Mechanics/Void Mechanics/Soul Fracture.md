<h1 class=shatt>Soul Fractures</h1>

> [!shatt|status|none]
> Soul Fractures are effects gained when struck by an attack that targets the soul. When struck for the first time, the unit gains Soul Fracture I. If the unit is struck again, it will advance to the next stage until IV.

<h2 class=shatt>Soul Fracture I</h2>

> [!shatt|status|none]
> <img src="soulfrac1.png" class=floatl> <span>Soul Integrity is reduced by 25%</span>
> 
> 
> Gain 2 ![[defdown.png|icon]] [[Defense Power Down]] and 2 ![[mendown.png|icon]] [[Mental Power Down]]   
>  ![[noctis.png|icon]] [[Noctis|Noctis Aether]] regeneration is increased by 25%
> 
> <i class="shatt no-b no-mix">"Something's off, feels like something's slipping your mind."</i>
