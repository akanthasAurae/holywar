<h1 class="stancebreak">Stancebroken</h1>

> [!stancebreak|status|none] 
>  <img src="stancebreak.png" class="floatl"><span>This unit has lost their footing, becoming completely susceptible to <img src="crit.png" class="icon"> <a data-href="Critical Hit" href="Critical Hit" class="internal-link">Critical Hits</a>, leaving them <img src="stun.png" class="icon"> <a data-href="Stun" href="Stun" class="internal-link">immobilized</a> until this effect expires or is removed.</span>
> 
> If this unit is struck by a ![[crit.png|icon]] [[Critical Hit]], lose two ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]] and completely remove this status. If struck by a regular hit instead, remove one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]] and decrease the stack by one.
> 
> **Max Stack:** 2
