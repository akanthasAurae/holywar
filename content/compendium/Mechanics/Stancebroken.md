<h1 class="stancebreak">Stancebroken</h1>

> [!stancebreak|status|none] 
>  <img src="stancebreak.png" class="floatl"><span>This unit becomes completely susceptible to <img src="crit.png" class="icon"><a data-href="Critical Hit" href="Critical Hit" class="internal-link">Critical Hits</a>.</span>
> 
> If this unit is struck by a ![[crit.png|icon]] [[Critical Hit]], lose two ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States]] and completely remove this status. If struck by a regular hit instead, remove one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States|Vital State]] and decrease the stack by one.
> 
> **Max Stack:** 2
