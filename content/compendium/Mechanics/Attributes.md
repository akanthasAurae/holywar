<h1 class=stats>Attributes</h1>

> [!stats|none]
> <span class=stats>Attributes</span> are the arbitrary representations of a character's life and experiences; be those victories, mental or physical ones, or utter losses, as well as tutoring, observation, research and merit.
> 
> In this sense, they can be understood as one's strengths and weaknesses, separated into five categories: <span class=stats>Body</span>, <span class=stats>Reflexes</span>, <span class=stats>Technicality</span>, <span class=stats>Intelligence</span>, and <span class=stats>Cool</span>. Every attribute starts at a base of 5 and can be increased up to 20. 
> 
> Throughout a character’s experiences, they gain attribute points which they can use to invest into one of the attributes. When investing a point, the character’s level increases equal to the attribute points invested.
> 
> <span class=stats>Body</span> encompasses one’s physical state; the endurance and capability to persist, determining one's ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]].<br>
> <span class=stats>Reflexes</span> encompasses one’s reaction time and focus; the instinctual coordination between brain and body, determining one's ![[stance.png|icon]] [[Stance]]. <br>
> <span class=stats>Technicality</span> encompasses one’s flexibility and technique in knowledge of their niches; determining one's ![[tactic.png|icon]] [[Tactic]]. <br>
> <span class=stats>Intelligence</span> encompasses one’s aptitude for highly complex or sophisticated solutions; determining one's ![[expertise.png|icon]] [[Expertise]]. <br>
> <span class=stats>Cool</span> encompasses one’s covertness and accuracy; the capacity to go unnoticed, determining one's ![[stealth.png|icon]] <span class=stealth>Stealth</span>.
> 
> The base rolls for all ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]], such as <span class=phys>Physical Actions</span> and <span class=cog>Cognitive Actions</span>, start at a **minimum** (floor) of 1, and **maximum** (ceiling) of 5. <span class=stealth>Stealth Actions</span> have their **minimum** (floor) locked at 1, and only its **maximum** (ceiling) can be increased.
> - <span class=phys>Physical Actions</span> include ![[combat.png|icon]] <span class=combat>Combat Actions</span>, such as <span class=combat>Melee</span> or <span class=combat>Ranged</span>, or ![[defense.png|icon]] <span class=defense>Defensive Actions</span>, such as <span class=defense>Guard</span>, <span class=defense>Evade</span>, or <span class=defense>Counter</span>.
> - <span class=cog>Cognitive Actions</span> include any kind of ![[mental.png|icon]] <span class=mental>Mental Actions</span>, such as <span class=cyber>Digital Actions</span> like hacking, or <span class=noctis>Magical Actions</span> such as spellcasting. 
>  - <span class=stealth>Stealth Actions</span> include any kind of ![[stealth.png|icon]] <span class=sneak>Sneak Actions</span>, such as backstabbing or lying.
> 
> Every unit additionally has <span class=sneak>Foresight</span>, a way of passively acknowledging their awareness to their surroundings. A <span class=stealth>Stealth Action</span> must roll higher than the target's <span class=sneak>Foresight</span> to succeed. When losing a <span class=sneak>Foresight</span> check, one becomes susceptible to a ![[crit.png|icon]] [[Critical Hit]]. *(not to be confused with <span class=poise>[On Crit]</span> which can be activated from ![[poise.png|icon]] [[Poise]])*
> 
> The rolling values for these actions can be increased accordingly via the formulas provided below, rounded down:
> 
> `Combat Floor = Body / 2`<br>
> `Combat Ceiling = Reflexes / 2`<br>
> `Mental Floor = Technical / 2`<br>
> `Mental Ceiling = Intelligence / 2`<br>
> `Stealth Ceiling = Cool`<br>
> `Foresight Threshold = Reflexes / 2`
