<h1 class="crit"> Critical Hit </h1>

> [!crit|status|none]
> <img src="crit.png" class="floatl"><span> <span class=crit>Critical Hits</span> are attacks that involve heavily wounding the enemy within a short period of time. Landing <img src="crit.png" class="icon"> <span class=crit>Critical Hits</span> naturally removes a <img src="vital.png" class="icon"> <span class=vital>Vital State</span> from the enemy no matter what.</span>
> 
> *(Not to be confused with <span class=poise>[On Crit]</span> which can be activated from ![[poise.png|icon]] [[Poise]])*
