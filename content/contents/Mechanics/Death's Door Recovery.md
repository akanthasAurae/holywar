<h1 class="ddrec"> Death's Door Recovery </h1>

> [!ddrec|status|none] 
> <img src="ddrec.png" class="floatl"><span> Gain 4 <img src="comdown.png" class="icon"> <span class="down"> Combat Power Down</span> and 4 <img src="defdown.png" class="icon"> <span class="down"> Defense Power Down. </span></span>
> 
> This status expires when the unit performs five ![[action.png|icon]] [[Action|Actions]] or more without entering ![[dd.png|icon]] [[Death's Door]] again.
> 
> If this unit enters ![[dd.png|icon]] [[Death's Door]] again, remove this status.
