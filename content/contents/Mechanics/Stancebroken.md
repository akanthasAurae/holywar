<h2 class="stancebreak">Stancebroken</h2>

> [!stancebreak|none] 
> <img src="stancebreak.png" class="floatl statusicon"> This unit becomes completely susceptible to ![[crit.png|icon]] <span class="crit">Critical Hits</span>.
> 
> If this unit is struck by a ![[crit.png|18]] <span class="crit">Critical Hit</span>, lose two ![[vital.png|18]] <span class="vital">Vital States</span> and completely remove this status. If struck by a regular hit instead, remove one ![[vital.png|18]] <span class="vital">Vital State</span> and decrease the stack by one.
> 
> **Max Stack:** 2
