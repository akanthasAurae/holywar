<h1 class="dd"> Death's Door </h1>

> [!dd|status|none] 
> <img src="dd.png" class="floatl"><span>The bells are tolling. When this unit takes fatal damage, a check <i>(DC 5)</i> is rolled towards the <b>Body.</b> </span>
> 
> If the unit succeeds, they permanently gain 4 ![[comdown.png|icon]] [[Combat Power Down]] and 4 ![[defdown.png|icon]] [[Defense Power Down]] for every successful check, for as long as this status is applied, and they continue to fight. The next DC is then increased by 5 *(stacks)*. If the unit fails, they will be incapacitated and can be <span class="dd">executed</span>.
> 
> If the unit recovers a ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vital States|Vital State]] while this status is active, it will expire and be replaced by ![[ddrec.png|18]] [[Death's Door Recovery]].
