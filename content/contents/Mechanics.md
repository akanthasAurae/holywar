## Humanity
> [!hum] Humanity
> ~={hum}**Humanity**=~ is what keeps one sane.
> 
> **~={hum}Humanity=~** has a base of **100** alongside a ~={hum}**Humanity Modifier**=~.
> <br>**`(Technical + Intelligence) / ((Body/5) + (Reflexes/5)) = Humanity Modifier`**
> <br>This modifier is then multiplied by the unit’s level; which will then be added to the base. 
> 
> **~={hum}Humanity=~** can be gained and lowered via actions that impact one’s psyche. If one reaches their **~={humlow}Low Humanity Threshold=~**, they will become unstable and will start showing proper Cyberpsychosis symptoms. If by chance the value reaches zero, then the unit will become indiscriminate and will be labelled as a Cyberpsycho.
> 
> **~={hum}Humanity=~** cannot be properly gained back, which means that once a character reaches their ~={humlow}**Low Humanity Threshold**=~, they will become more of a liability to their team. Characters will have to eventually take breaks, stepping away from duty if they want to regain their **~={hum}Humanity=~** and avoid Cyberpsychosis.
> 
> **~={cyber}Cyberware=~** influences the amount of **~={hum}Humanity=~** one has, reducing its maximum depending on how much **~={cyber}Cyberware=~** one has installed.
> > [!plus]+ Humanity increasing factors
> > **Resting**
> > <br>• Gain 40 **~={hum}Humanity=~** for every non-participated Mission (If character is restless and seeks action, the gain may be reduced)
> > 
> > **Healing**
> > <br>• Gain 4 ~={hum}Humanity=~ per ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ recovered
> > <br>• When healing while ![[downed.png|18]] ~={downed}Downed=~, gain 16 ~={hum}Humanity=~, but ~={humlow}Humanity Loss=~ is increased by x1.2 until rest
> > <br>• When healing out of ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death's Door=~, gain 22 ~={hum}Humanity=~, but ~={humlow}Humanity Loss=~ is increased by x1.5 until rest 
> > 
> > **Clash Win**
> > <br>•

- Gain 1 **Humanity**
- Gain 2 **Humanity** if winning a second clash a row
- Lose 4 **Humanity** if winning a clash after winning more than three clashes a row

### ~={vital}Vital State=~
> [!vital|none]
> ![[vital.png|floatl|74]] Every character begins with 2 ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~, representing their overall resilience and capacity to remain combat-effective. ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~ function as a character’s primary health measure.
> 
> ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~ are lost when a character suffers excessive damage or when they are explicitly removed by status effects or skills. When a character is reduced to 0 ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~, they are either ![[downed.png|18]] ~={downed}Downed=~ or placed into ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death’s Door=~, depending on the unit’s highest attribute.
> 
> Units that have higher **Body** than **Reflexes** enter ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death’s Door=~ when their ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ is depleted, and units that have higher **Reflexes** than **Body** become ![[downed.png|18]] ~={downed}Downed=~ instead.
> 
> A character gains additional ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~ through investment in **Body**:
> +1 ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ at **Body 15**
> +1 ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ at **Body 20**
> +1 additional ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ for every **10 Body** thereafter
> *tags: #vitalstate #body #reflexes #mechanics*

### ~={stance}Stance=~
> [!stance|none] 
> ![[stance.png|floatl|74]] Every character begins with 1 ![[stance.png|18]] ~={stance}Stance=~, representing balance, posture, and combat footing.
> 
> ![[stance.png|18]] ~={stance}Stance=~ is lost when a character takes heavy damage, is struck by posture-breaking attacks, or is affected by statuses that disrupt stability, such as ![[tremor.png|18]] ~={tremor}[[Statuses#~={tremor}Tremor=~|Tremor]]=~. When a character's ![[stance.png|18]] ~={stance}Stance=~ is reduced to 0, they become ![[stancebreak.png|18]] ~={stancebreak}Stancebroken=~.
> 
> +1 ![[stance.png|18]] ~={stance}Stance=~ at **Reflexes 20**
> +1 additional ![[stance.png|18]] ~={stance}Stance=~ for every **15 Reflexes** thereafter.
> *tags: #stance #reflexes #mechanics *

### ~={dd}Death's Door=~
> [!dd|none] 
> ![[dd.png|floatl|74]] When this unit takes fatal damage, a check *(DC 5)* is rolled towards the **Body.**
> 
> If the unit succeeds, they permanently gain 3 ![[comdown.png|18]] ~={down}Combat Power Down=~ for as long as this status is applied, and they continue to fight. The next DC is then increased by 5 *(stacks)*. If the unit fails, they will be incapacitated and can be ~={dd}executed.=~
> 
> If the unit recovers a ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ while this status is active, it will expire and be replaced by ![[ddrec.png|18]] ~={ddrec}Death's Door Recovery=~.
> *tags: #deathsdoor #combatpowerdown #mechanics *

### ~={ddrec}Death's Door Recovery=~
> [!ddrec|none] 
> ![[ddrec.png|floatl|74]] Gain 2 ![[comdown.png|18]] ~={down}Combat Power Down=~ and 2 ![[defdown.png|18]] ~={down}Defense Power Down=~.
> This status expires when the unit performs five ![[action.png|18]] ~={action} Actions=~ or more without entering ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death’s Door=~.
> 
> If this unit enters ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death’s Door=~ again, remove this status.
> *tags: #deathsdoor #deathsdoorrecovery #mechanics *

### ~={downed}Downed=~
> [!downed|none] 
> ![[downed.png|floatl|74]] This unit is ~={downed}incapacitated=~ and cannot be targeted. If every other ally is ~={downed}incapacitated=~ or ~={dd}killed=~, this unit becomes targetable and can be ~={dd}executed=~, or they may naturally die.
> *tags: #downed #deathsdoor #mechanics *

### ~={stancebreak}Stancebroken=~
> [!stancebreak|none] 
> ![[stancebreak.png|floatl|74]] This unit becomes completely susceptible to ![[crit.png|18]] ~={crit}Critical Hits=~. If this unit is struck by a ![[crit.png|18]] ~={crit}Critical Hit=~, lose two ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~ and completely remove this status. If struck by a regular hit instead, remove one ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ and decrease the stack by one.
> 
> **Max Stack:** 2
> *tags: #stance #reflexes #criticalhit #mechanics *

### ~={guardbreak}Guardbroken=~
> [!guardbreak|none] 
> ![[guardbreak.png|floatl|74]] This unit gais 5 ![[comdown.png|18]] ~={down} Combat Power Down=~ and cannot use Defense skills. Expires at the end of the unit's ![[action.png|18]] ~={action}Action=~. 
> *tags: #guardbroken #combatpowerdown #mechanics *

### ~={crit}Critical Hit=~
> [!crit|none]
> ![[crit.png|floatl|74]] ~={crit}Critical Hits=~ are attacks that involve heavily wounding the enemy within a short period of time. Landing ![[crit.png|18]] ~={crit}Critical Hits=~ naturally removes a ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ from the enemy no matter what.
> 
> *(Not to be confused with ~={poise}[On Crit]=~ which can be activated from ![[poise.png|18]] ~={poise}[[Statuses#~={poise}Poise=~|Poise]]=~)*

### ~={shatt}Shattered=~
> [!shatt|none]
> ![[shattered.png|floatl|74]] The last stage of your damaged soul, you are vulnerable; your soul's integrity is compromised.
> Gain 10 ![[comdown.png|18]] ~={down} Combat Power Down=~ and ![[defdown.png|18]] ~={down} Defense Power Down=~.
> When ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ is lost due to a Physical Attack, lose two additional ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital States=~. When ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~ is lost due to a Status Effect Attack, lose an additional ![[vital.png|18]] ~={vital}Vital State=~.
> ![[dd.png|18]] ~={dd}Death’s Door=~ and ![[downed.png|18]] ~={downed}Downed=~ are not triggered while this status is active.
> *tags: #soulfracture #combatpowerdown #defensepowerdown #vitalstate #deathsdoor #downed #shattered*
