> [!biography]+ known history
> *born and raised in the cruel, cold mountains of Kakunodate, sharing a birthplace with Ryūji Suzuki; there was birthed a woman now known by the title of the Devil's Snake. at a ripe 24 years of age, the very same man who she'd sworn to bear heirs of— now dishonoured by the village that once cradled them as children— has brought her to Allheim, the city that never sleeps, only a year after taking Sylo under his wing. seemingly, as well, the only woman capable of deciphering what goes on behind his iconic and ageless poker face, picking at his detachment as easily as prying loose threads from a ragged dress.*
>    
> *she is highly independent, having instead made the choice of remaining by his side rather than having her hand forced, and has adapted frighteningly fast to the culture of Hoshikage. Although she remains without implants or cyberware, she has quickly caught on to how the city functions on a deeper basis and learned the local language extremely rapidly, going from dressing akin to an Edo period wanderer to an elite woman in little to no time. Her adaptability is something to keep an eye on.*
>    
> *she still has no system records as of yet, but seems to be neutral towards the yakuza her partner has involved himself with, and her behavior suggests she is accustomed to observing and manipulating people, especially those in lower or higher positions of political or socioeconomic power than her (few as those are). speculation indicates she may have been involved in decades-old cold wars in Japan. she carries on her person three weaponry, if necessary: twin scimitars, Tsumi and Bitoku (Sin and Virtue), and her bow, Shizukesa (Serenity). multiple victims of her weaponry have been found with venom as a cause of death, covert and silent, leaving no proof behind.*
