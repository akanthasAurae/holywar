<h1 class=charge>Charge</h1>

> [!charge|status|none] 
> <img src="charge.png" class="floatl"> <span><span class=charge>Charge</span> builds through three levels — <span class=charge>Minor</span>, <span class=charge>Moderate</span> and <span class=charge>Major</span>. Any <img src=charge.png class="icon"> <span class=charge>Charge</span> gained while in <img src=charge.png class="icon"> <span class=charge>Major Charge</span> changes this effect into <img src=fullcharge.png class="icon"> <span class=charge>Fully Charged</span> and it cannot decay passively.</span>
> 
> ![[charge.png|icon]] <span class=charge>Charge</span> is a resource and it can only decay when being consumed by Skill effects. ![[charge.png|icon]] <span class=charge>Charge</span> can be triggered into ![[overload.png|icon]] <span class=overcharge>Overcharge</span> with the right equipment.
> 
> When triggered, the unit releases all stored energy and becomes ![[overload.png|icon]] <span class=overcharge>Overcharged</span> for three ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]]. While ![[overload.png|icon]] <span class=overcharge>Overcharged</span>, gain +4 ![[comminup.png|icon]] [[Combat Power Up]] and automatically fulfill all Charge-based conditionals.
> 
> As long as ![[overload.png|icon]] <span class=overcharge>Overcharged</span> is active, this unit cannot gain ![[charge.png|icon]] <span class=charge>Charge</span>.
