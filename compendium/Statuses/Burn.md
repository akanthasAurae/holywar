<h1 class=burn>Burn</h1>

> [!burn|status|none] 
> <img src="burn.png" class="floatl"> <span><span class=burn>Burn</span> builds through three levels — <span class=burn>Minor</span>, <span class=burn>Moderate</span> and <span class=burn>Major</span>. Any <img src="burn.png" class="icon"> <span class=burn>Burn</span> applied while in <img src="burn.png" class="icon"> <span class=burn>Major Burn</span> immediately triggers <img src="igni.png" class="icon"> <span class=igni>Ignition</span>.</span>
> 
> When triggered, the unit becomes ![[scorch.png|icon]] <span class=scorch>Scorched</span> for two ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]], and will consume one ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]] at the end of each of their ![[action.png|icon]] [[Actions]]. If ![[igni.png|icon]] <span class=igni>Ignition</span> is triggered again while ![[scorch.png|icon]] <span class=scorch>Scorched</span> is still active, the duration is refreshed.
> 
> If ![[scorch.png|icon]] <span class=scorch>Scorched</span> causes the unit to lose their final ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]], they will advance to ![[dd.png|icon]] [[Death's Door]]/![[downed.png|icon]] [[Downed]].
