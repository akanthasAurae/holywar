<h1 class=vital>Vitality</h1>

> [!vital|status|none]
> <img src="vital.png" class="floatl"><span> Every character begins with 2 <img src="vital.png" class="icon"> <span class=vital>Vitality</span>, representing their overall resilience and capacity to remain combat-effective. <img src="vital.png" class="icon"> <span class=vital>Vitality</span> functions as a character’s primary health measure.</span>
> 
> ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> is lost when a character suffers excessive damage or when it is explicitly removed by status effects or skills. When a character is reduced to no ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span>, they are either ![[downed.png|icon|20]] [[Downed]] or placed into ![[dd.png|icon|20]] [[Death's Door]], depending on the unit’s highest attribute.
> 
> Units that have higher <span class=body>Body</span> than <span class=reflex>Reflexes</span> enter ![[dd.png|icon|20]] [[Death's Door]] when their ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> is depleted, and units that have higher  <span class=reflex>Reflexes</span> than <span class=body>Body</span> become ![[downed.png|icon|20]] [[Downed]] instead.
> 
> A character gains additional ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> through investments in <span class=body>Body</span>:<br>
> +1 ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> at <span class=body>15 Body</span><br>
> +1 ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> at <span class=body>20 Body</span><br>
> +1 additional ![[vital.png|icon|20]] <span class=vital>Vitality</span> for every <span class=body>10 Body</span> thereafter
