<h1 class=exp>Expertise</h1>

> [!tac|status|none]
> <img src="expertise.png" class=floatl> <span><span class=exp>Expertise</span> is the manifestation of one's <span class=intel>Intellectual</span> prowess, used to enhance **potency-based** Status Effects such as <img src=blood.png class=icon> <a data-href="Bloodloss" href="Bloodloss" class="internal-link">Bloodloss</a> and <img src=charge.png class=icon> <a data-href="Charge" href="Charge" class="internal-link">Charge</a>.</span>
> 
> These Status Effects have conditionals that can be fulfilled the higher your <span class=intel>Intelligence</span> is, inflicting more of their <span class=minus>negative effects</span>, or depleting more ![[vital.png|icon]] [[Vitality]]. These conditionals are divided in multiple tiers, starting at **tier 1**.
> 
> The limit of how much a Status Effect's potency can be upgraded depends on the Status Effect itself. Some effects have a limit of **tier 3**, while others can reach up to **tier 5**.
> 
> *(Additionally, to unlock higher tiers the character must research their own effects to learn them better and thus achieving such results.)*
> 
> A character can gain higher tiers, and thus, better potency, through the investment in <span class=intel>Intelligence</span>, increasing the tier for every <span class=intel>10 Intelligence</span>.
