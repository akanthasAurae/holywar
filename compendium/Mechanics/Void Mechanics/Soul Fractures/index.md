# <span class=soul>Soul Fractures</span>

^bc3945

> [!soul|status|none]
> <img src=soulfrac.png class=floatl> <i class="no-b no-mix soul">"Erasing you, erasing me; one fragment at a time."</i>
> 
> ![[soulfrac.png|icon]] <span class=soul>Soul Fractures</span> are effects gained when struck by an attack that targets the soul, also known as ![[soul.png|icon]] [[Soul|SOUL Attacks]]. Using soul-healing magic, these fractures can be mended before they go too far.
> 
> When struck for the first time, the unit gains ![[soulfrac1.png|icon]] [[Soul Fracture I]]. If the unit is struck again, it will advance to the next stage, up to ![[soulfrac4.png|icon]] [[Soul Fracture IV]]. From there on, the soul will become ![[shattered.png|icon]] [[Shattered]] once struck again.
