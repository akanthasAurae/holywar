<h1 class="shatt"> Shattered </h1>

> [!shatt|status|none]

> Whatever remains of your soul, a hollow core.
> 
> Gain 15 ![[minmaxdown.png|icon]] [[Final Power Down]].
> ![[dd.png|icon]] [[Death's Door]] and ![[downed.png|icon]] [[Downed]] are completely nullified while this Status is active. There's no soul, no will to live. If the body dies, it <span class="dd no-mix">dies</span>.
> 
> If a unit with this status effect lives for long enough, they end up corrupting into a dangerous enemy called the ‘Shattered’; if the unit has their soul repaired, they will not corrupt. These enemies remain permanently ![[shattered.png|icon]] [[Shattered]].
