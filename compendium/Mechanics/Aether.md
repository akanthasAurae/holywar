<h1 class=aether>Aether</h1>

> [!aether|status|none] 
> <img src="aether.png" class="floatl"> <span>The physical manifestation of <span class=aether>mana</span> and what is modernly labelled <i>magic</i>. Unattainable in Solstice for humans without a <b>Reawakened Core</b>. Is otherwise known as <span class= aether>Aether Points</span>.</span>
> 
> Can be attained through certain items, passives and conditionals, and often is spent to make additional effects occur that can otherwise not be achieved by default.
